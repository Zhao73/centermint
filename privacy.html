Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

Overview

CenterMint ("we", "our", or "the app") is a card centering measurement tool. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect and how we handle it.

Data We Handle Locally

Card photos and centering measurements stay on your device. CenterMint processes card images locally for centering measurement and does not automatically upload those images, measurements, scan history, or manual adjustment samples to our servers.

  • Photos & Camera: Used solely to capture or import card images for centering measurement. Images are not automatically sent off your device. You can choose to export or share images and reports using the app's sharing tools.
  • Device Motion: Used only for the real-time level indicator during photo capture. This data is not stored or shared.
  • Local Correction Records: New installations keep manually adjusted card images and frame positions on the device by default. Earlier choices to disable this are preserved. You can turn recording off, export records, or delete them in Data & Privacy. These records support later review; saving them does not automatically train or update a detection model. They are not sent to Firebase or any other remote training service.

Third-Party Services

The app may use the following third-party services:

  • Apple StoreKit: For processing in-app subscriptions. Payments are handled entirely by Apple. We do not have access to your payment information.
  • Google AdMob: For displaying advertisements in the free version. AdMob may collect device identifiers, approximate location, advertising data, product interaction, crash data, performance data, and other diagnostic data for advertising, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention according to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google User Messaging Platform: For presenting and storing advertising consent choices where required. This SDK may process approximate location, product interaction, and performance data for consent and app functionality.

Advertising Choices

Ad personalization and tracking behavior may depend on your region, device settings, and consent choices. You can manage advertising privacy controls in iOS Settings and, where shown, in the app's consent prompt.

Subscriptions

CenterMint Pro is available as a monthly or yearly auto-renewable subscription. Payment is charged to your Apple ID account. Subscriptions automatically renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage subscriptions in your device Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.

Data Storage

Scan history, saved records, and local training samples are stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage. This card data is not accessible to us and is not automatically transmitted to any external server. Files you choose to export or share are handled by the destination you select.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
