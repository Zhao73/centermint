Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 22, 2026

CenterMint ("we", "our", or "the App") is developed by JIAPENG Zhao. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use CenterMint.

Information We Collect

CenterMint is designed with privacy in mind. We process card images entirely on your device. We do not upload, transmit, or store your photos on any server.

Camera & Photo Library Access

The App requests access to your camera and photo library solely to capture or import card images for centering analysis. These images are processed locally on your device and are never sent to external servers.

Data Storage

Scan results and saved records are stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage. This data is not accessible to us or any third party.

Third-Party Services

The App may use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — for displaying advertisements in the free version. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data according to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple StoreKit — for processing in-app purchases. Payment information is handled entirely by Apple.

In-App Purchases

CenterMint offers optional premium features through in-app purchases processed by Apple. We do not collect or have access to your payment information.

Analytics

We do not use any analytics SDKs. We do not track your usage behavior, location, or personal information.

Children's Privacy

CenterMint does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except as required by law.
