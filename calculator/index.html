From border widths to a useful ratio

Left share = left ÷ (left + right) × 100. Top share = top ÷ (top + bottom) × 100. The other side is the remainder. For example, left 3 mm and right 2 mm produce 60.0 / 40.0. Reversing the inputs produces 40.0 / 60.0; the amount of imbalance is the same.

Measure both widths at comparable positions, away from rounded corners. Keep front and back measurements separate. A perspective-distorted photograph can change the apparent widths: straighten the view before measuring. The preview is illustrative and does not reproduce a particular card.

One decimal place is a display choice, not proof of measurement accuracy. This calculator does not assign a grade.
