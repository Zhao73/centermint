Card centering FAQ

What is card centering?

Centering is how evenly the printed image sits within the card's borders, expressed as a left/right and top/bottom ratio such as 55/45. A 50/50 card is perfectly centered; the larger the first number, the more off-center the card.

What centering does a PSA 10 need?

PSA's published scale lists 55/45 or better on the front for Gem Mint 10, 60/40 for Mint 9, 65/35 for NM-MT 8 and 70/30 for NM 7. Back allowances are looser. CenterMint shows which PSA threshold your measured ratio meets.

What are the BGS and CGC centering standards?

BGS Pristine 10 requires 50/50; BGS 9 allows 55/45 to 60/40; BGS 8 allows 65/35. CGC Gem Mint 10 allows 55/45, CGC 9 60/40 and CGC 8 65/35. These are front-side limits from the graders' published scales; the in-app guide links to each source.

How do I measure card centering with an iPhone?

Photograph the card flat under even light or import a scan. CenterMint proposes the outer card edge and the printed inner border; check both frames with the magnifier, adjust any edge, and the app calculates the left/right and top/bottom ratios instantly.

Does it work for Pokémon, One Piece, Yu-Gi-Oh!, Magic and sports cards?

Yes. Any card with a printed inner border can be measured. Full-art and borderless cards may lack a measurable inner frame; CenterMint tells you when a frame needs manual confirmation.

Is CenterMint affiliated with PSA, BGS or CGC?

No. CenterMint is an independent tool that estimates centering only. It does not grade surface, corners, edges or authenticity, and a professional grade may differ.
