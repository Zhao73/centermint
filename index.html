Card centering before grading

Check the frame before you check the result. Measure card centering, fine-tune each edge and keep front and back scans together.

Open in the App Store

CenterMint helps collectors measure the left/right and top/bottom border ratios of trading cards and sports cards. Photograph a card or import an image, inspect the suggested outer and inner frames, then confirm or adjust the edges before reading the result.

How CenterMint helps

  • Inspect the card edge and printed inner border directly on the photo.
  • Adjust individual edges with a magnifier and fine controls.
  • Compare centering ratios with the reference thresholds in the app.
  • Keep front and back scans together in a searchable local collection.
  • Share a centering image with your measurements.

Optional Pro tools

An optional CenterMint Pro purchase unlocks detailed reports, batch image and CSV exports, saved editing presets and an ad-free experience. Available plans and local prices are shown in the app. Subscription plans renew automatically until canceled; manage them in your Apple account settings.

What to check

Card analysis runs on your device. No account is required. Card photos and measurements are not automatically uploaded. Sharing and exporting are actions you choose.

The app supports English, Japanese, Korean, Vietnamese, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese.

Detection suggestions need review. Lighting, sleeves, perspective and card design can affect the detected frame. Full-art cards may not have a measurable printed inner border. CenterMint estimates centering only, not surface, corners, edges, authenticity or a professional grade. It is an independent tool and is not affiliated with Pokémon, PSA, Beckett or CGC.
