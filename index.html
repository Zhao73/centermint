Card centering FAQ

What is card centering?

Centering compares the left/right and top/bottom border widths. A 50/50 ratio means equal widths on that axis. Both 40/60 and 60/40 are equally off-center; keep the direction labels when comparing measurements.

What centering does a PSA 10 need?

PSA's published Gem Mint 10 centering guideline is approximately 55/45 or better on the front and 75/25 on the reverse. Centering alone cannot establish a grade: surface, corners, edges and other criteria also matter. Check the current official scale before submitting.

What are the BGS and CGC centering standards?

BGS and CGC have separate scales. Check the card category and whether a rule concerns the front or back. CGC's published 60/40 for Mint 9 and 65/35 for NM/Mint 8 are specified for sports and non-sports cards; do not apply those figures as universal TCG rules. CenterMint is an independent measurement aid, not a grading decision.

How do I measure card centering with an iPhone?

Photograph the card flat under even light or import a scan. CenterMint proposes the outer card edge and the printed inner border; check both frames with the magnifier, adjust any edge, and the app calculates the left/right and top/bottom ratios instantly.

Does it work for Pokémon, One Piece, Yu-Gi-Oh!, Magic and sports cards?

Cards with visible, consistent inner reference borders can be measured. Automatic suggestions need checking, especially with glare, sleeves and full-art designs. If a card has no measurable inner reference, a guessed rectangle cannot establish accurate centering.

Is CenterMint affiliated with PSA, BGS or CGC?

No. CenterMint is an independent tool that estimates centering only. It does not grade surface, corners, edges or authenticity, and a professional grade may differ.
